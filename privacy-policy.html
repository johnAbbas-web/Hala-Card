Hala Card Website Privacy Policy · Last updated 25 October 2026

01

Who we are

This policy explains how Hala Card handles the personal data you give us through our website (halacard.co) and our waitlist.

Hala Card is operated by Hala Card Company (شركة هلا كارد), registered in the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia under Commercial Registration No. 7054122010, based in Madinah. We are the controller of your personal data under the Saudi Personal Data Protection Law (PDPL).

For any privacy question or request, email info@halacard.co.

02

What we collect

When you join our waitlist, we collect:

Your name Where you are from (country or city) Your email address

We do not collect payment details, sensitive data or anything else through the website.

03

Why we use it

To tell you when the Hala Card app launches and send you launch offers.

Legal basis · Your consent

To understand which regions our visitors come from, in combined form only.

Legal basis · Legitimate interest in planning our service

We do not sell your personal data or share it for other companies' marketing.

04

Your consent

We email you only if you tick the consent box when you sign up. The box is never ticked for you, and we keep a record of when you gave consent.

You can withdraw consent at any time, free of charge, by using the unsubscribe link in any email or by emailing info@halacard.co. We stop sending messages without undue delay.

05

Who we share it with

  • Hostinger, which hosts our website and stores waitlist sign-ups on our behalf.
  • Authorities, if Saudi law requires it.

No one else receives your data.

06

Where your data is stored

Your data is stored with Hostinger, whose servers may be located outside the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia. When data is stored outside the Kingdom, we transfer only what is needed and only under the safeguards required by the PDPL. If Saudi rules require us to keep data inside the Kingdom, we will do so.

07

How long we keep it

We keep your waitlist details until 12 months after the app launches, or until you unsubscribe, whichever comes first. Then we delete them.

08

Security and breaches

We protect your data with encrypted connections (HTTPS) and limit access to the people who need it.

If a breach could harm you, we will notify the Saudi Data and AI Authority (SDAIA) within 72 hours of becoming aware of it, and tell you without undue delay where it could cause you damage.

09

Your rights

You have the right to:

Know how and why we use your data (this policy)
Get a copy of your data
Correct your data
Have your data deleted
Withdraw your consent at any time

Email info@halacard.co from the address you signed up with. We reply within 30 days. If you are not satisfied with our answer, you can complain to the Saudi Data and AI Authority (SDAIA) within 90 days of the issue.

10

Children

You must be 18 or older to join the waitlist, or have a parent or guardian's permission. If we learn we have collected a child's details without that permission, we delete them.

11

Cookies

Our website uses only the cookies it needs to work, such as remembering your language choice. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies. If we add analytics or advertising tools, we will ask for your consent first and update this policy.

12

Changes and contact

We may update this policy when our service or the law changes, and will post the new version here with its date.

Questions about your data?

Write to us any time — we reply within 30 days.
