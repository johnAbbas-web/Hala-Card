01

Who we are

Hala Card Company (شركة هلا كارد), CR No. 7054122010, Madinah, Kingdom of Saudi Arabia, is the controller of your personal data under the Saudi Personal Data Protection Law (PDPL). Contact: info@halacard.co.

02

What we collect

  • Your first name and email address
  • Your country, if you choose to give it
  • Your choices: acceptance of the offer terms, marketing consent (yes/no) and the time you gave them
  • Reservation details: status, reservation code, place in the first 500, and whether the gift has been claimed
  • Technical security data: a one-way scrambled (hashed) version of your IP address, kept for up to 24 hours to stop abuse, and a random code stored on your phone so your pass reopens

04

Sharing your pass

If you tap “Share”, the link you send shows only your first name on a pass preview. It never shows your email, country, or reservation code.

05

Who receives your data

  • Hostinger, which hosts our website, database and email on our behalf
  • Authorities, if Saudi law requires it

Hostinger's servers may be outside the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia. Any transfer is limited to what is needed and made under the safeguards required by the PDPL.

06

How long we keep it

We keep your reservation until 12 months after Hala Card launches, then delete it, unless you used the discount, in which case the record of that purchase is kept as long as Saudi law requires. Marketing consent ends when you unsubscribe. Unconfirmed registrations are deleted 30 days after the offer closes.

07

Your rights and choices

You can access, correct, or delete your data, and withdraw marketing consent at any time, free of charge. Use the “Manage my details” link in any of our emails to stop marketing or cancel and delete your reservation immediately, or email info@halacard.co. We reply within 30 days. You may complain to the Saudi Data and AI Authority (SDAIA).

08

Cookies and storage

This page uses no advertising or tracking cookies. It stores a random pass code in your browser's local storage so your pass reopens on this phone. You can clear it at any time in your browser settings.

09

Children

You must be 18 or over, or have a parent or guardian's permission, to register.
