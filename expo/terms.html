01

The offer

Hala Card Company (شركة هلا كارد), CR No. 7054122010, Madinah, Kingdom of Saudi Arabia (“Hala Card”, “we”) offers visitors to Sierah Expo 2026 the chance to reserve an exclusive Hala Card gift, together with access to Hala Card partner benefits at up to 10 places across Madinah, available after Hala Card officially launches (planned for December 2026). The exact gift and partner benefits will be confirmed in your launch invitation.

02

Who can take part

  • You must be 18 or over, or have permission from a parent or guardian.
  • You must register through the official Sierah Expo QR code page and confirm your email address using the link we send you.
  • The gift is limited to the first 500 people to confirm their email address. Registrations confirmed after the 500 places are taken join our launch list without the gift.
  • One reservation per person and per email address. Duplicate, automated, or false registrations may be cancelled.
03

What the reservation is

  • Your digital Explorer pass is a personalised reservation and discovery preview. It is not an activated Hala Card pass, a voucher, or a payment instrument, and cannot be used at partner venues.
  • After launch we will email you an invitation to purchase an eligible pass using your unique reservation code.
04

Claiming your gift

  • One gift per reservation, claimed through Hala Card's official channels using your unique reservation code.
  • The gift must be claimed within 30 days of the launch invitation email being sent to you. Unused reservations expire after that date.
  • The code can be used once only. It is personal, non-transferable, has no cash value, and cannot be exchanged, sold, or combined with other offers unless we say otherwise.
  • The email address used to claim may need to match the one you registered with.
  • Partner benefits are provided by each partner and are subject to their own conditions and availability.
05

Partner experiences

Experiences shown in “Discover Madinah” are a preview and are subject to partner confirmation. Partners, benefits, and opening times may change before or after launch. Any partner offer will be shown in the Hala Card app with its own conditions.

06

Changes and cancellation

We may change or withdraw this offer if required by law, if the launch date changes, or for reasons outside our control. If we do, we will tell registered visitors by email. We may cancel reservations we reasonably believe were obtained by fraud or misuse. You can cancel your reservation at any time using the “Manage my details” link in any of our emails.

We use your details to manage this offer as described in our Sierah Expo Privacy Notice.
